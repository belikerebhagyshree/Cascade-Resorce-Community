🌍 Why Community Resource Sharing Matters

Better resource use: Useful items can be shared instead of remaining unused.

Support for people in need: Community members can request items they cannot easily obtain.

Fairer allocation: Allocation rules can help identify which requests can be satisfied and which needs remain unmet.

Cascade allocation research: Our planned extension will explore whether a sequence of compatible resource exchanges can satisfy needs that direct matching cannot satisfy.
